How It Works

Connect in under
60 seconds.

Follow these simple steps and your phone will be on your screen in no time.

1
Enable Developer Options
On your Android phone, go to Settings → About Phone and tap "Build number" 7 times to unlock Developer Options.
2
Turn On Wireless Debugging
Go to Settings → Developer Options → Wireless Debugging and switch it on. Make sure your phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi.
3
Pair with Pairing Code
Tap "Pair device with pairing code" on your phone, then enter the IP, Pair Port, and 6-digit code into WirelessConnect.
4
Open Live Screen & Control
Hit "Pair & Connect" — your device appears in the sidebar. Click "Open Live Screen" and start mirroring!

Troubleshooting

Having trouble? We've got you.

Here are the most common issues and how to fix them.

🔒 "Pairing failed. Check the 6-digit code." error

The pairing code expires within 60–90 seconds. If you were slow entering the details, the code expired.

✅ Fix:

  1. On your phone, tap "Pair device with pairing code" again to get a fresh code.
  2. Quickly fill in the new IP, Pair Port, Pairing Code, and Debug Port in the app.
  3. Hit "Pair & Connect" immediately.
📱 "error: protocol fault (couldn't read status message)"

This is a generic ADB error on macOS. It happens when the app tries to connect to your phone's IP and Port, but the connection fails or the phone doesn't respond. ADB shows this "protocol fault" instead of a direct Connection Refused error.

✅ Fixes:

  1. Wrong Port (Most Common): The main Wireless Debugging screen shows an IP and Port. But when you tap "Pair device with pairing code", the popup shows a different port! You MUST use the port shown in the popup for pairing.
  2. Not on the same Wi-Fi: Ensure both your Mac and Android phone are on the exact same Wi-Fi network. Some routers have "AP Isolation" or "Guest Network" enabled, which blocks devices from communicating. Try a mobile hotspot to test.
  3. macOS Firewall & Local Network Permission: If your Mac's firewall is on (System Settings > Network > Firewall), try disabling it temporarily. If you are on macOS 15 (Sequoia), ensure the app has Local Network access in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  4. Check Network Reachability: Open your Mac Terminal and type ping [YOUR_PHONE_IP]. If it says "Request timeout", your Mac cannot reach the phone at all.
💻 PC on Ethernet, Phone on Wi-Fi (Different Networks)

If your PC is connected via an Ethernet cable and your phone is on Wi-Fi, they can connect only if they are on the same router (same subnet). If they are on different networks, they cannot communicate natively. Here are the best workarounds:

✅ Solutions:

  1. Virtual LAN (Software - Recommended): Install a free Virtual Private Network tool like Tailscale or ZeroTier on both your PC and Phone. They will assign a virtual IP to your phone (e.g., 100.x.x.x). Enter this virtual IP in WirelessConnect to connect from anywhere!
  2. PC Mobile Hotspot (Best Performance): If your PC has a Wi-Fi card, turn on "Mobile Hotspot" in your PC's network settings. Connect your Android phone directly to the PC's hotspot. This ensures a direct connection with 0ms latency!
  3. USB Wi-Fi Adapter (Hardware): If your desktop PC lacks a Wi-Fi card, you can buy a cheap USB Wi-Fi dongle ($5). Plug it in, enable Mobile Hotspot from the PC, and connect your phone directly.
  4. USB Tethering (Temporary Bridge): Connect your phone to the PC via USB temporarily and turn on "USB Tethering" in your phone's network settings. This creates a direct local network between them. You can then connect via the app using the new tethering IP.
